OpenMRG: an interactive talk, rebuilt from the paper, not by the authors

Paper: J. C. M. Andersson, J. Olsson, R. (C. Z.) van de Beek, J. Hansryd,
"OpenMRG: Open data from Microwave links, Radar, and Gauges for rainfall
quantification in Gothenburg, Sweden", Earth Syst. Sci. Data 14, 5411-5426,
2022, https://doi.org/10.5194/essd-14-5411-2022 (CC BY 4.0).

Data: the OpenMRG data set v1.1, https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7107689,

the 28 July 2015 event and the summer statistics in this talk (inside
dist/deck.js) are derived from it and are shared under CC BY-SA 4.0.

Map: coastline, lakes and rivers (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0,
https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright. The derived map data inside
