Optical music recognition works well on clean, engraved scores — and falls apart on
everything else: handwritten manuscripts, dense orchestral pages, non-standard notation.
This project builds a universal OMR framework that holds up on visually complex notations,
and explores real-time human–GenAI interaction for controllable music generation on top of it.

## why it matters

Most music GenAI systems treat scores as an afterthought. Making notation machine-readable
at scale unlocks controllable generation: a musician edits the score, the model responds in
real time.

## status

Active — building the recognition framework and prototyping the interaction loop.
