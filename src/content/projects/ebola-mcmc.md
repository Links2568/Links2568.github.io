Epidemic records are full of holes: onset and removal times are partially observed and
censored. This project develops a Bayesian data-augmentation MCMC analysis for Ebola
outbreak data that treats the missing event times as latent variables and samples them
jointly with the model parameters — recovering the transmission picture the raw records
can't show directly.
