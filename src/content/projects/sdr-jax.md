Wearable sensors produce multilevel data — thousands of observations per patient, dozens of
patients, structure at every level. This project applies sufficient dimension reduction to
wearable data from bone marrow transplant patients, and re-implements the core SDR
algorithms in JAX: **7,000× faster** than the reference implementation, which turns
overnight runs into interactive analysis.
